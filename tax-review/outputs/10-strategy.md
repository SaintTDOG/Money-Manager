## 1. The strategy in one line

**Do nothing irreversible. The Thai tax at stake is small; the real work is (a) getting
Thai-side compliant for 2025 if a return was due, (b) protecting the two companies from a Thai
PE exposure, and (c) confirming the NZ residence anchor. Stay NZ / dual-resident for now and
use the DTV simply to live in Thailand lawfully — it buys residence, not a tax break.**

residence is probably "sticky" via a family home (PPOA); and ceasing NZ residency is
irreversible and likely ineffective. The disproportionate risk is at **company level**.

## 2. Priority actions

### P1 — URGENT (this month): resolve the 2025 Thai filing question
- **Time-sensitive.** If Theo was Thai-resident in 2025 (≥180 days — currently *borderline*)
  **and** remitted assessable post-2024 income into Thailand in 2025, a **PND 90 was due
  31 March 2026** — now ~3.5 months late, with **s.27 surcharge at 1.5%/month** accruing.
- **Do:** (1) obtain the **official Thai Immigration movement record** to settle the 180-day
  count definitively; (2) engage a **Thai adviser now** to assess whether a 2025 return was
  required and, if so, make a **voluntary disclosure** to cap penalties.
- **But note:** tax is only on *assessable income remitted*. If 2025 remittances trace to
  **pre-2024 savings (Por 162 pool)** or capital, or fall under allowances, the tax may be
  **nil** even if a return is technically required. Quantify before assuming a liability.

### P2 — Confirm the NZ residence anchor (decisive, low-cost)
- Confirm the **NZ family home** is available to Theo (ASB shows V P / H G Holmes — likely
  parents). If available, Theo very likely **remains NZ tax-resident** under the permanent
  place of abode test (Van Uden) regardless of days — and the treaty tie-breaker leans to NZ.
- **This is fine, and probably preferable:** NZ is a clean filing home; it avoids an
  irreversible cessation for a trivial saving.

### P3 — Company-level Thai PE / CM&C (the biggest real risk — affects Hadleigh & Henry)
- Have the **AU + Thai advisers** assess whether HA Pty Ltd and T&H Collective have a **Thai
  permanent establishment / central management and control** because Theo directs them from

- **Manage governance with advice:** keep high-level board decisions documented as made
  outside Thailand, keep co-directors genuinely active, avoid recasting Theo as the sole
  Thailand-based decision-maker.

### P4 — Keep extraction simple
- **Stay on contractor fees.** Do **not** convert to director's fees or dividends — worse AU
  treatment (s 128B / PAYG) and it *aggravates* the company PE risk (P3), with Part IVA
  exposure on any re-papering.
- **Close the structural gap:** put a **written services agreement** in place between Theo and
  each of HA and T&H (currently none exists — G-14).

### P5 — Legitimate forward remittance planning (with advice)

  - **Do NOT rely on the draft two-year exemption** — not enacted (`03` §3).
- **LTR visa** is not realistic now (income below the ~USD 80k threshold; sole trader, not a
  qualifying employee) — so there is no visa-based exemption route on current facts.
