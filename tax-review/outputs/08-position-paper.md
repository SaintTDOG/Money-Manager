## 3. The recommended scenario, with reasoning

**Recommendation: adopt S3 (treat the current position as dual-residence to be resolved by
the treaty tie-breaker) as the working basis, and do NOT elect S2 (deliberate NZ cessation)
now.** In sequence: **stabilise and evidence first; decide the irreversible question last.**

Reasoning (drawing S1–S4 in `06`):

1. **The decision is irreversible-class; the evidence is absent.** Electing S2 means
   abandoning any NZ PPOA, exiting NZ GST (deemed supply, `02` §5) and accepting Thai
   remittance taxation. One does not take an irreversible step on an empty evidence base.
2. **The personal tax at stake is small and unverified.** On the brief's own figures the
   *personal* Thai exposure is at most low-thousands of AUD, and after RT-04 the honest base
   is "near-nil to the ATM line only" — the CBA/T&H receipts are probably not Thai remittances
   at all. A small, unverified saving does not justify an irreversible step.
3. **NZ may tax Theo anyway (PPOA).** If a NZ home remains available, ceasing "residency" by
   day count fails (Van Uden) and S2's premise collapses — Theo would be NZ-resident and
   exposed to NZ reassessment, interest and penalties.
4. **The largest real risk is at company level, not personal.** The Thai PE / central
   management and control exposure of HA and T&H (`06` §1.1) may already be live and could
   **dwarf** Theo's personal tax — and it falls on Hadleigh Vernall and Henry Holmes. That
   risk is present in every scenario and is aggravated, not solved, by an S2/S4 Thai-director
   restructure.
5. **Nothing here should lean on the draft Thai exemption.** It is **not enacted** (`03` §3,
   re-verified). Any plan that only works under it is a bet on non-existent law.

**S3 as the working basis** buys the right things: it is reversible, it forces the evidence to
be gathered, and it resolves the tie-breaker on facts rather than on a premature election. S1
(remain NZ-resident) is the natural fallback if the tie-breaker or the PPOA points to NZ. S2
and S4 are decisions to be *considered by the advisers* only **after** the evidence exists and
the company-level exposure is sized — not now.
