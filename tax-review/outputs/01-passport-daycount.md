## 0. Method, reliability and health warning (read first)

- The images are phone photos taken in low light; many spreads are **upside down**, stamps
  **overlap**, and some ink is faint. Every entry below carries a confidence flag:
  **[clear]**, **[likely]**, **[uncertain]**.
- **Only foreign stamps exist.** Thailand, Malaysia, China, UAE, Indonesia and (older)
  Cambodia/Philippines stamp the passport. **New Zealand and Australia do NOT appear** — as
  expected (NZ citizens and AU e-gates are generally not ink-stamped). So this passport
  **cannot** directly evidence NZ or AU days; those require flight records (gap G-02).
- Thai day counting: **a part-day of presence counts as a full day** for the 180-day test.
- This paper reads entries/exits and infers presence *between* a Thai entry and the next Thai
  exit. Where the counterpart stamp is missing or unclear, the period is flagged.
- **Redaction:** passport number and date of birth are held on file but omitted here for
  privacy. Identity confirmed from the bio page: NZ passport, holder THEODORE SCOTT HOLMES,
  New Zealand citizen, valid to 21 Sep 2028.
